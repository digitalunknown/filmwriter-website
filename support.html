Filmwriter

Support

Last updated: October 14, 2026

Filmwriter is a one-time iPhone purchase for logging film rolls, cameras, lenses, and frame settings, and for writing those notes into scan metadata (such as EXIF) when you export.

Contact

Email hi@filmwriter.app for product questions, bug reports, App Store help, or privacy requests. Include your iOS version and Filmwriter version when you can (Settings in the app).

Common topics

  • No account: Filmwriter does not use Sign in with Apple or a Filmwriter login. Your library stays on your devices.
  • iCloud: Optional backup or sync uses your Apple ID iCloud. Turn it off in Settings if you want the app local-only.
  • Export and backup: Use Settings to export a full JSON library backup, or export frames so notes land in the image file. Destinations (Files, Photos, share sheet) are yours to choose.
  • Purchases and refunds: Billing is handled by Apple through the App Store. For purchase or refund questions, use Apple’s reportaproblem.apple.com or your App Store purchase history.
  • Privacy: See the Privacy Policy.

Response time

This is a small indie app. Aim is a reply within a few days. Urgent App Store review questions get priority.
